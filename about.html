About Hale Noor Online Quran Academy

هالة نور أكاديمية لتعليم القرآن الكريم والعلوم الإسلامية

Founded with the sacred mission to make authentic Quranic education accessible, engaging, and affordable for Muslim families living anywhere in the world.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, 1-on-1 personalized Quran and Islamic education that respects each student's unique learning pace. We bridge the gap for Muslim families residing in English-speaking and western nations by offering fluent, compassionate, certified scholars right into their homes.

Our Vision

To cultivate a new generation of Muslims who not only recite the Holy Quran with pristine Tajweed, but also understand its timeless message, love the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), and carry noble Islamic character (Akhlaaq) wherever they go.

Our Educational Standards

How we guarantee excellence in every single lesson

Strict Tutor Screening

Only 1 out of 10 tutor applicants pass our stringent recitation, Tajweed, English communication, and background check standards.

Child-Friendly Pedagogy

Strict zero-harshness policy. We use positive encouragement, gamified quizzes, and patience to keep young minds enthusiastic.

Quality Supervision

Our Academic Head regularly reviews recorded class samples and evaluates teacher punctuality and Tajweed adherence.

Parent Communication

Direct WhatsApp coordination channel. Monthly attendance, recitation scorecards, and easy rescheduling.
