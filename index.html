Curriculum & Courses

Comprehensive Quranic Courses for All Ages

Structured step-by-step learning path from basic Arabic letters to advanced Tajweed, Hifz, and Tafseer. Click "View Syllabus" for module breakdowns.

Beginners & Kids
القاعدة النورانية

Noorani Qaida with Tajweed

The essential first foundation. Master Arabic alphabet recognition, correct Makharij (pronunciation points), Harakaat, Tanween, and Sukoon.

  • Letter recognition & joining rules
  • Correct points of articulation (Makharij)
  • Digital colored Qaida worksheets
Most Popular
تلاوة القرآن بالتجويد

Quran Reading with Tajweed (Nazra)

Fluent recitation of the Holy Quran from Surah Al-Fatihah to An-Nas with rules of Madd, Waqf (stopping signs), Ghunnah, and Qalqalah.

  • Fluent reading without hesitation
  • Complete rules of practical Tajweed
  • Beautiful melodious recitation style
Full & Partial Hifz
حفظ القرآن الكريم

Hifz-ul-Quran (Memorization)

Systematic daily memorization under the guidance of experienced Huffaz. Includes Sabaq (new lesson), Sabqi (recent Juz), and Manzil (old revision).

  • Proven memory retention techniques
  • Daily revision tracking sheet for parents
  • Option to memorize selected Surahs or 30 Juz
Youth & Adults
ترجمة وتفسير القرآن

Quran Translation & Tafseer

Understand the Divine Message directly. Word-by-word Urdu/English translation, context of revelations (Shan-e-Nuzool), and practical life lessons.

  • Word-by-word Quranic vocabulary
  • Tafseer of profound & daily recited Surahs
  • Bilingual instruction (Urdu & English)
Essential Foundation
الدراسات الإسلامية والأدعية

Islamic Studies, Namaz & Duas

Step-by-step practical Salah (Namaz), 6 Kalimas, daily Masnoon Duas, Seerah of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), and Islamic manners (Adaab).

  • Correct Wudu & Namaz with translation
  • Daily routine Duas (waking, eating, sleeping)
  • Islamic moral character & manners building
Language Mastery
قواعد اللغة العربية للقرآن

Quranic Arabic & Grammar

Learn Arabic morphology (Sarf) and syntax (Nahw) tailored specifically to unlock the grammatical beauty and meaning of the Quranic text.

  • Core verb root conjugation patterns
  • Key 80% Quranic vocabulary breakdown
  • Grammatical analysis of famous Surahs
